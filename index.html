Our story

Pure Dairy began in 1998 with twelve cows and a simple promise: sell only what we would feed our own children. Today our farms near Ludhiana care for over 1,200 cows, and every bottle still carries the date it was milked.

Mission

Make honest, nutritious dairy affordable for every home.

Vision

Our farms

Cows graze on open pasture and eat organic fodder grown on-site. Milking is hygienic and automated, and milk is chilled to 4°C within minutes.
