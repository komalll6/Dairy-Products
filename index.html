Our Story

Koms Dairy began in 1998 with twelve cows and a simple promise: serve only what we would feed our own family. Today, our farm cares for healthy cows with organic grass feed, delivering purity directly to homes across Jalandhar Cantt.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver unadulterated, nutritious dairy and golden A2 Ghee directly to every dining table.

👁️ Our Vision

A sustainable farm ecosystem where animals are nurtured with care and families thrive on genuine purity.

Modern Hygiene & Care

Our cattle graze freely on organic pastures. Automated touchless milking ensures spotless hygiene, while milk is chilled immediately to 4°C to preserve freshness.
