Our Story: From 12 Cows to Punjab's Favorite Dairy

Pure Dairy started over two decades ago near Ludhiana with twelve cows and a firm promise: deliver unadulterated milk as pure as mother nature intended. Today, our automated chilling unit maintains milk at 4°C within 15 minutes of milking to preserve raw vitamins, probiotics, and rich flavor.
